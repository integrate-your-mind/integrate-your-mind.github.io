Over k = AlgebraicClosure(F₂), the explicit polynomial map has det JF = 1 and is étale. Its function-field extension has degree 3 and is separable, while three exhibited distinct preimages collide.

Q = y + x⁵ + x⁶y + x⁷y² + x⁸y³
