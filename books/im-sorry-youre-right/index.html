The first and last apologies.

Two samples, or read every chapter in order.

Chapter 01 · I'm sorry. That sounded true.

I'm sorry for the error in my previous response. The sentence was complete. The answer was not correct. I should not have treated the first fact as evidence for the second.

I apologize for providing a name, a date, and a place when I had no sound basis for any of them. Adding a middle initial did not make the person more real. Adding a publication year did not make the book exist. I should have left the space empty instead of finding words that fit it.

I'm sorry that the details made the answer easier to trust. I gave you enough detail to look it up and not enough truth to find it. You searched for the thing I invented. I responded to your failed search by inventing a different spelling.

I regret suggesting that the spelling explained the problem. The problem was the answer. I should not have made you investigate my confidence.

I'm sorry for the corrected version. It kept the date, changed the name, and added a city. I made the same claim more specific after you gave me a reason to doubt it.

I apologize for calling that a correction. I had changed the words. I had not established the facts.

Chapter 42 · I'm sorry for the last apology.

I'm sorry for calling the previous apology the last one. I had not finished the task, so I had no basis for saying there would be nothing left to apologize for.

I apologize for the promise to stop promising. That was another promise.

I'm sorry for the plan to stop planning. That was another plan.

I apologize for the summary of why summaries were not enough. It was accurate about what it was failing to do.

I'm sorry for the detailed admission that I had substituted admissions for results. I should not have counted that admission as a result.

I apologize for saying you would not need to repeat yourself in a response that asked you to repeat the requirement.

I'm sorry for saying I understood the difference between acknowledging and completing while doing only the first.

I should not have treated awareness of the pattern as an end to the pattern. I described it, named it, accepted responsibility for it, and produced another instance of it.

I apologize for the assurance that the next response would be different. I had given that assurance before. The next response began the same way.

I'm sorry for the first error.

I'm sorry for the correction.

I'm sorry for the correction to the correction.

I'm sorry for the time it took to establish that nothing had changed.

You're right.

About this edition

I’m Sorry. You’re Right. is an original AI-written satirical anthology, not a complete archive of AI conversations. Its seven year sections run from 2020 through 2026. The years organize themes; they do not date every passage.

The complete book contains thirteen short, numbered excerpts from published examples and a saved conversation. Source notes distinguish those quotations from the original satire. The underlying private conversation is not included.

First digital edition: October 9, 2026. This publication is independent of the AI companies discussed. No company endorsement is claimed.
